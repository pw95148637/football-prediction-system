# J_SPEC v2 — frozen instrument prompt (2026-07-09)

> Supersedes v1 for locks created AFTER 2026-07-09. Change is PROCESS-
> motivated, not score-motivated (the freeze rule): v1 forced every
> deviation through a single lever (d_eff, the strength axis), so J could
> not express a STYLE/TEMPO view at all — the mechanism taxonomy contains
> `tactical_stylistic_matchup` but v1 gave it no corresponding output.
> Analyst-J has always legally held tempo views (m95 pilot: lambda -10%
> with reasons); v2 gives the runner-era instrument the same vocabulary.
> Registered in the experiments registry as j_spec_v2. Everything in v1
> not repeated here is unchanged (doctrine, inputs, forbidden reasoning,
> decomposition rule, JSON discipline).

## What v2 adds: a second, orthogonal lever

Factors now come in two kinds:

1. **strength factor** (v1 unchanged): `"lever": "d_eff"`,
   `d_eff_shift` signed toward `direction`. Moves win/loss mass along the
   Elo axis.
2. **tempo factor** (new): `"lever": "tempo"`,
   `total_goals_multiplier` in [0.75, 1.25] (conservative band), with the
   same evidence/absorption/magnitude fields. Scales BOTH lambdas
   multiplicatively — expresses "this match will be more open / more
   shut" (low block confirmed, incentive to kill the game, extreme heat,
   a pressing chaos matchup). It changes draw probability and the whole
   score distribution WITHOUT taking a side.

Rules for tempo factors:
- Same doctrine bar as any deviation: the tempo view must come from
  information the market may not have fully priced, with the absorption
  case stated. "The market total looks wrong vs our curve" is BANNED
  (that is the market-simulator failure mode on the totals axis — the
  6-match curve-vs-market totals tension is an open instrument question,
  not a per-match J input).
- Direction and hedge must not share a source, across levers too: the
  same fact may not both shift d_eff toward the favourite and lower
  tempo to fatten the draw you just thinned. One fact, one lever.
- H1-class hypotheses (鐵桶和局) remain OUTSIDE J until their
  pre-registered n>=6 threshold is met — a confirmed 5-x-1 low-block
  lineup is a legitimate tempo fact; "underdogs park the bus in
  knockouts" as a base rate is not yet.

## Output changes

- probs_90 must now be COMPUTED from the goal model: apply strength
  shifts to d_eff -> lambdas from the calibrated curve -> apply tempo
  multiplier -> Dixon-Coles grid (adopted rho) -> 1x2 + qualify. State
  the resulting lambdas in a new field `"j_lambdas": {"home": x, "away":
  y}`. (v1 read 1x2 off the ordered logit, which cannot see tempo; with
  two levers the goal model is the only consistent probability source.
  The two disagree by <1pp at zero tempo shift — verified OOT: DC-grid
  1x2 RPS 0.16225 vs ordered-logit 0.16267.)
- Factor schema: `mechanism_type`, `lever` ("d_eff"|"tempo"),
  `d_eff_shift` (required when lever=d_eff), `total_goals_multiplier`
  (required when lever=tempo), `direction` ("home"|"away"|"none" — tempo
  factors use "none"), `evidence_refs`, `absorption_case`,
  `magnitude_note`.
- Empty factors still means J = M exactly, stated in basis.

## Unchanged from v1 (binding)

Inputs (M + validated bundle + this spec, nothing else); deviations only
from market-may-not-have information; Elo-vs-market abstractions banned;
no price targets / timing / certainty narratives; k-sample runner
mechanics; versioning freeze rule.
